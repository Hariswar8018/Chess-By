## **📱 App Overview**

![fv21OvY07Sck_1024_500](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/168038f0-cde5-4fd8-a4a8-88274f172a41)


**Chess By** is a feature-packed app designed for chess enthusiasts. Whether you're looking for chess players, clubs, tournaments, or even teachers, this app provides everything you need. It also integrates with popular chess platforms for score tracking and offers OTP verification for secure wins.

---

## **🛠️ Features**

![1731054872270](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3dd5ba4e-4de3-43f7-b160-18ccb1557ba7)

- **Chess Player Finder:** Easily find chess players in your area or online.  
- **Chess Club Finder:** Discover chess clubs near you or online.  
- **Chess Tournament View:** Stay updated on upcoming tournaments and events.  
- **Club and Player Distance Finder:** Find chess clubs or players based on distance from your location.  
- **Online Chessboard:** Play chess directly on the app with an interactive chessboard.  
- **Verify Wins with OTP:** Secure win verification through OTP for added security.  
- **Schedule Chess Game Appointments:** Book appointments with players or clubs for chess games.  
- **Score Integration:** Get your score from platforms like [Chess.com](https://www.chess.com) and [Lichess.org](https://www.lichess.org).  
- **Find Chess Teachers:** Locate chess teachers to improve your skills.
